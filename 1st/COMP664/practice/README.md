# COMP664 open-ended practice

Each numbered course section has three separate question files (one per level) and two separate answer sheets, all in its own section folder. Work through Level 1, then close it before attempting the later levels. Questions differ across levels.

- **Level 1:** guided practice with formulas and step-by-step worked answers in the question file.
- **Level 2:** questions with relevant formulas, but no answers in the question file; solutions are in `level_2_answers.md`.
- **Level 3:** questions only, with no supplied formulas, hints, or answers; solutions are in `level_3_answers.md`. Problem data may include mathematical expressions needed to define the task.

All logs are natural unless stated otherwise. Vectors and matrices use the convention specified in each problem. Use unrounded intermediate values; numerical answers may differ slightly by rounding. Population variance is used in normalization exercises. GB/MB are decimal; MiB is 2^20 bytes. Simplified models and idealized estimates are identified in their questions.

Coverage follows the 13 numbered MT1/MT2 course folders and their Markdown summaries/study notes. Section 2.5 includes the efficiency material in its `note.md`. These are newly authored practice exercises, not official exam questions. Supplemental derivations (for example receptive-field arithmetic) support the course concepts.
